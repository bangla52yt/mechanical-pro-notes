## 2. Applicability & Content Depth Taxonomy

### 2.1 Plant Applicability Classifications
- **[Common]:** Present in virtually 100% of industrial textile/garment washing plants.
- **[Plant-Dependent]:** Presence depends on plant washing capacity, garment processing type (denim, non-denim, dyeing), or plant layout.
- **[Optional/Specialized]:** Advanced automation, specialized chemical processing, or sustainable zero-liquid discharge (ZLD) additions.
- **[Unknown - Owner Confirmation Required]:** Specific machine models, ratings, or local configurations in the owner's facility requiring owner input.
