## 3. Executive Project Summary: What is Mechanical Pro Notes?
- **What it is:** A **Personal Mechanical Knowledge & Plant Maintenance Reference App** built initially and exclusively for the project owner's personal use on their own Android smartphone.
- **What it is NOT:** It is NOT a public product, NOT for the Google Play Store, has NO ads, NO monetization, NO multi-user system, and NO DRM.
- **Core Domain Focus:**
  1. Complete mechanical systems, piping, utilities, and transmission for textile/garment washing plants (16 System Families A through P, 64 validated equipment records).
  2. A dedicated, top-level industrial Boiler module (Gas-fired and Jhut/fabric waste-fuel boilers + 14 verified calculations + The Boilers Act, 2022 + Bangladesh Boiler Rules, 2025).
  3. General mechanical engineering principles bridging theoretical science to practical shop-floor maintenance.
- **Owner Context:** The project owner operates exclusively from an **Android smartphone**. The owner does not write code, edit configs, or run terminal commands. The AI assistant acts as Principal Architect, Technical Writer, and Knowledge Architect.
- **Language Protocol:** Executive briefings, reports, and questions in **Bengali**; technical documentation, filenames, and code in **English**.
