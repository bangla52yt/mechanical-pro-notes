## 1. Source Hierarchy & Evidence Verification Standard
To prevent technical fabrication and ensure shop-floor reliability, all technical specifications, equations, safety rules, and operating limits in Mechanical Pro Notes must derive from recognized engineering authorities:
1. **Tier 1: Current Statutory Legislation & Official International Standards** (Current national laws, ASME, ISO, BS EN, NFPA).
2. **Tier 2: Primary Manufacturer Engineering Manuals & Datasheets** (Spirax Sarco, SKF, Grundfos, Atlas Copco, Cleaver-Brooks, Alfa Laval).
3. **Tier 3: Standard Academic & Professional Engineering Handbooks** (Shigley, Perry, Cengel, Mobley).
4. **Tier 4: Verified Industrial Maintenance Field Practice** (Documented empirical data tagged with plant classification).

Every entry that lacks a confirmed physical publication is marked **`[E. UNVERIFIED INFORMATION]`** or **`[F. UNKNOWN OR MISSING INFORMATION]`** until verified.

## 3. Statutory vs Engineering Code Hierarchy (Mandatory Distinction)
To eliminate ambiguity in plant engineering and compliance:
1. **Statutory Legislation (Legally Binding in Bangladesh):**
   - **The Boilers Act, 2022 (Act No. 06 of 2022):** Mandatory legal requirement (commenced 11 April 2022; repealed the 1923 Act under Section 54). Operating an unregistered or uncertified boiler (`Sec 18/19`, `Sec 38`) or operating without a licensed attendant (`Sec 9`, `Sec 36`) carries penalties of up to 2 years imprisonment, 2 lakh BDT fine, or both.
   - **Bangladesh Boiler Rules, 2025 (`S.R.O. No. 279-Law/2025`, published 29 June 2025):** Framed under `Section 52` of Act No. 06 of 2022 (`69` Rules). `Rule 3` sets statutory lifespan caps (`10/15/30/60 yr`, up to `+10 yr` Board-approved extension) and inspection/certification rules. Verify exact statutory compliance procedures against the official Bangladesh Gazette before legal reliance.
2. **International Engineering Construction Codes:**
   - **ASME BPVC Section I & BS EN 12953:** Mandatory for manufacturer pressure vessel calculations, material selection (ASTM A516 Gr 70), weld radiographic inspection, and safety valve flow sizing.
3. **Safety & Operational Standards:**
   - **NFPA 85 / EN 298:** Standardizes burner management systems (BMS), pre-purge cycles, and flame safeguard interlocks.
   - **ISO 20816-3:2026:** Standardizes *in situ* broad-band vibration evaluation for coupled industrial machines **`>15 kW`** and **`120–30,000 r/min`** on rigid or flexible supports (excluding rotodynamic pumps per `ISO 10816-7`, reciprocating machines per `ISO 10816-6`/`20816-8`, rotary PD compressors/blowers, and machines `<=15 kW` or `<120 r/min`).
4. **Manufacturer Instructions & Recommended Practices:**
   - OEM maintenance manuals provide machine-specific torque limits, bearing greasing schedules, sub-15 kW vibration baselines, and part replacement intervals.
